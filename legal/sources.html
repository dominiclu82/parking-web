What we do to the data 我們對資料做了什麼

We publish exactly what the sources give us, with these corrections:我們原樣呈現來源提供的內容,僅作下列修正:

  • New Taipei coordinates are published in TWD97 TM2 and are converted to WGS84.新北市座標以 TWD97 二度分帶發布,已換算為 WGS84。
  • Taoyuan publishes its coordinates with the field names wgsX and wgsY transposed; we correct this.桃園市的 wgsX/wgsY 欄位名稱互換,已修正。
  • Taipei uses -9 to mean "this value is not provided". We show that as Unknown 未知, never as 0.臺北市以 -9 表示「未提供此項」,我們顯示為未知而非 0。
  • Rates are parsed from each city's free-text field into an hourly figure; where a site charges different rates by time of day, a range is shown.費率由各市的自由文字欄位解析為每小時金額;分時段不同價者顯示為區間。
  • New Taipei on-street bays are aggregated by road segment. The meaning of its status codes has not been confirmed against official documentation, so treat on-street figures as indicative.新北市路邊車格依路段彙總。其狀態碼含意尚未經官方文件確認,路邊數字請視為參考值。

Update pipeline 更新機制

Each source is fetched independently every 10 minutes and published to data.h-peak.com. If a source cannot be reached, the last successful result for that source is reused and flagged as stale — both in the Settings tab and at the top of the map. A failure in one city never removes the other cities' data.各來源每 10 分鐘獨立抓取一次,合併後發佈於 data.h-peak.com。某個來源抓不到時,沿用該來源上一次成功的結果並標示為過期 —— 同時顯示於「設定」分頁與地圖上方。單一縣市失敗不會讓其他縣市的資料消失。
