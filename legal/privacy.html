In one line 一句話 — no account, no tracking, no advertising, and your location never leaves your device.不需帳號、不做追蹤、沒有廣告,您的位置永遠不會離開您的裝置。

1. What we collect 我們蒐集什麼

Nothing. The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no crash reporter. We operate no user database and hold no personal data about you.沒有。本 App 沒有登入機制、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告 SDK、沒有當機回報。我們不營運使用者資料庫,不持有您的任何個人資料。

2. Location 位置資訊

With your permission, the app reads your device location to sort nearby car parks by distance. It is used on the device only. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is never stored off your device. You may decline the permission and set your location manually by tapping the map instead; the app remains fully usable.經您同意後,App 會讀取裝置位置以依距離排序附近停車場。該位置僅在裝置上使用,不會傳送給我們或任何第三方,也不會儲存於裝置之外。您可以拒絕授權,改以點地圖手動指定位置,App 功能完全不受影響。

3. Stored on your device 存在您裝置上的資料

The app keeps the following in your browser's or app's local storage, so that it works offline and remembers your preferences:為了離線可用並記住您的偏好,App 於瀏覽器/App 的本機儲存區保存下列資料:

  • The most recent parking dataset最近一次取得的停車資料
  • Your saved places您的常用地點
  • Language, day/night and text-size settings語言、日夜與字級設定
  • Any location you set manually您手動指定過的位置

We cannot read any of it. Deleting the app, or clearing website data, removes all of it.我們讀不到其中任何一項。刪除 App 或清除網站資料即可全部移除。

4. Network requests 對外連線

Request 連線Destination 目的地What is sent 送出的內容
Parking data 停車資料data.h-peak.comA plain request for a public JSON file. No identifiers.單純索取一份公開 JSON,不帶任何識別資訊。
Map tiles 地圖圖磚OpenStreetMapThe map area you are viewing. As with any web request, they see your IP address.您正在瀏覽的地圖範圍。與任何網路請求相同,對方會看到您的 IP。

There are no other outbound connections.除此之外沒有任何對外連線。

5. Reporting a problem 回報問題

The Private report button does not send anything by itself. It opens your own mail app with the message pre-filled, including a short diagnostic block (app version, data timestamps, which sources responded, the map centre, browser user-agent). You can read and edit all of it before sending, and the message is sent by you, from your own mail account.「私下回報」本身不會送出任何東西。它只是開啟您自己的郵件軟體並預先填好內容,其中包含一段簡短診斷(App 版本、資料時間、各來源是否正常、地圖中心點、瀏覽器 UA)。送出前您都看得到也可以修改,信件由您以自己的信箱寄出。

The Community button opens a public LINE group. Anything you post there is visible to other members.「社群討論」會開啟公開的 LINE 群組,您在其中的發言其他成員都看得到。

6. Children 兒童

The app is not directed at children and collects no age information.本 App 不針對兒童設計,亦不蒐集任何年齡相關資訊。

7. Your rights 您的權利

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase on your behalf. Everything is on your device and under your control.由於我們未持有您的任何個人資料,無從代為查詢、更正、攜出或刪除。所有資料都在您的裝置上,由您自行控制。

8. Changes 變更

If this policy changes, the date above is revised and the change is noted in the app's changelog.本政策如有變更,將更新上方日期並於 App 的更新日誌記載。
