The app shows parking locations, live space counts and rates assembled from open data published by the Taipei, New Taipei and Taoyuan city governments. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong: sensors fail, feeds go down, and a space shown as free may be taken by the time you arrive. Never treat it as a reservation or a guarantee.本 App 顯示的停車位置、即時空位數與費率,來自臺北市、新北市、桃園市政府公開的開放資料。資料可能延遲、不完整或錯誤:感測器會故障、來源會中斷,顯示為空的車格也可能在您抵達時已被停走。請勿將其視為預約或保證。

"Unknown" is not zero 「未知」不等於沒有空位 — where a source does not publish a live count, the app shows 未知 / Unknown. It does not display that as 0, because that would wrongly suggest the car park is full.當來源未提供即時空位時,App 顯示「未知」而非 0 —— 顯示成 0 會讓您誤以為停車場已滿。
