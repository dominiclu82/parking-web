Getting help 取得協助

Tap the version number at the bottom-right of the app to open the changelog. Two buttons are at the top of it:點 App 右下角的版號開啟更新日誌,上方有兩個入口:

  • 💬 Community 社群討論 — a public LINE group; you may post anonymously. Open 開啟公開的 LINE 群組,可匿名發言。
  • 🔒 Private report 私下回報 — opens your mail app with diagnostics pre-filled. Only we see it. support@h-peak.com開啟您的郵件軟體並預填診斷資訊,只有我們看得到。

Why does a car park show "Unknown"? 為什麼顯示「未知」?

That source does not publish a live space count for it — some car parks only publish their location and rates, with no sensors. We deliberately do not show that as 0, because it would wrongly look full.代表該來源沒有提供那一筆的即時空位 —— 有些停車場只公開位置與費率,沒有接感測器。我們刻意不顯示成 0,那會讓它看起來像滿了。

How often does the data update? 資料多久更新一次?

Every 10 minutes. The Settings tab shows the last update time and whether each source responded. Sources and their own publishing intervals are listed on the Data Sources page.每 10 分鐘。「設定」分頁會顯示最後更新時間與各來源是否正常;各來源自身的更新頻率見資料來源頁。

The count was wrong when I arrived 我到的時候數字不對

Live counts come from the city's sensors and can lag, or a space can be taken between the data being published and you arriving. The app is an aid for choosing where to head, not a reservation.即時數字來自各市的感測器,可能延遲;車位也可能在資料發佈到您抵達之間被停走。本 App 是幫您決定往哪裡開的輔助,不是預約。

Why only Taipei, New Taipei and Taoyuan? 為什麼只有北北桃?

Every city publishes in a different format, coordinate system and status encoding, so each one has to be integrated separately. More will be added.每個縣市的開放資料格式、座標系統與狀態編碼都不同,必須逐一接入。之後會陸續增加。

Does it cost anything? 需要付費嗎?

No. There is no sign-in, no in-app purchase, no subscription and no advertising. See the Terms.不需要。沒有登入、沒有內購、沒有訂閱、沒有廣告,詳見服務條款。

Can you see where I am? 你們看得到我的位置嗎?

No. Your location is used on your device only and is never transmitted. See the Privacy Policy.看不到。您的位置僅在裝置上使用,從不傳送。詳見隱私權政策。
